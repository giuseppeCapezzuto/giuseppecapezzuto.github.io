Drop & Evolve

A creature-collecting merge puzzle. Drop a creature into the jar; two of the same kind fuse into the next one up an eleven-step chain, from a speckled egg to a full-grown horse. Everything you make is recorded in a codex that survives every run.

No ad ever interrupts a run. Free, with no purchases and no accounts.
